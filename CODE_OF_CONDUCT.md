# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep critiques about the work. Do not
post secrets, household viewing data, or copyrighted media captures.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may warn, hide, or ban. Report conduct issues through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the GitHub maintainer profile for
[4m4n5/mango](https://github.com/4m4n5/mango).

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
